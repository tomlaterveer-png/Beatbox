Beatbox Live test

Beatbox and hear drums right away. Proof of concept: kick, snare, hi-hat. Use headphones, or the drums from the speaker will trigger themselves.

K

Teach: tap Teach, then make that sound 3 times. Teaching all three makes it much more accurate. It's saved on this phone.

Delay

–

Measure plays 5 clicks through the speaker and times how long they take to come back into the mic. Take headphones off for this, and stay quiet. Recognising a sound adds about 9 ms on top.
