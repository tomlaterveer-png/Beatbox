Record like a tape deck: pick a track, press REC or DUB (it blinks), then Play. Already playing? It starts at the top of the next loop.

REC wipes the track and records one loop, then plays it back. In Session mode it keeps going and the song grows until you press REC again. DUB keeps looping and adds every pass until you press DUB again.

Track settings: the tab on the left of the tracks, or swipe right on a track name. Song settings: this sheet, swipe up from the bottom.

Drums: pick one row and everything you beatbox lands in that row. Pick several rows (or “All drums”) to have your sounds sorted into them.

Bass and lead: hum the line; every note lands in the key. Higher notes sit higher. Tap a note to change it, tap an empty spot to add one. ▾ folds a track.

Record output captures everything you hear into a WAV file, until you tap OUT at the top. Headphones keep the beat out of the mic; wired works best.
